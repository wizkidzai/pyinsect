Terms of Use

These are the rules for using Pyinsect (this website, its bug reports, accounts, and the Innvadex game). By using Pyinsect, you agree to them. If you're under 13, read them with a parent or teacher. Our Privacy Policy explains what we save and why.

1. Pyinsect can be wrong

Pyinsect's AI makes guesses, and guesses can be wrong. It isn't an expert. Don't use it alone to decide something important, like cutting down a tree or using bug spray. If you think you found an invasive insect, you can also tell your state's agriculture department.

2. Send real reports

  • Only report photos you took yourself, of real insects or trees.
  • Don't send fake reports, AI-made pictures, or photos of people.
  • Don't go onto someone else's land without asking, and don't touch insects that might sting.

3. Your photos

Your photos are still yours. When you send a report, you let our team keep it and use it to check the find, to tell people who protect trees, and to show it on the map (only the bug's name, the month, and a rough area: never the photo). You can ask us to delete it.

4. Accounts and the game

  • Keep your password secret. Don't use someone else's account.
  • Be kind. Names (for Team members who pick their own) must not be rude or be someone's real name.
  • Play fair: no cheating, and no trying to break or overload our servers.
  • Our team can block an account or a device that breaks these rules, for a while or forever.

5. Free, as it is

Pyinsect is free, and it's a kids' team project. We do our best, but it comes "as is": it might have bugs (the computer kind), stop working for a while, change, or close. We can't promise it will always be right or always be online, and we aren't responsible for problems that come from using it.

6. Pictures and code

Some pictures on Pyinsect belong to other people and are used under their licenses (see "Photo credits" on the home page). Sign in with Google follows Google's terms.

7. Changes and questions

We may change these rules. When we do, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Ask a grown-up on our team, or open an issue on our GitHub page (don't write anything private there).
