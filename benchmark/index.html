      How to read this
      • Accuracy: out of 100 photos, how many the first guess would get right.
      • Speed: the faster AI gets 100%. An AI that takes twice as long gets 50%.
      • Cost: dollars spent on all the photos. AIs that run on your device cost $0.
      • Tokens: small pieces of words and pictures that a chatbot AI reads and writes. K means thousand. Only OpenRouter counts them here, so the others show 0.
      • Graphs: accuracy, speed and cost added up, photo by photo. A lower speed line is faster. Both graphs use the same scale.
      • Pyinsect is our own model. Use photos it didn't learn from, and at least 30 of them.
      • The other AI sees the whole photo. Pyinsect cuts around the insect first.
      • With CLIP, your photos stay on your device. With OpenRouter, they are sent to OpenRouter and the model's company, and the speed includes the internet.
