Privacy Policy

The short version

  • We never ask for your email, your real name, or your phone number.
  • We don't sell anything about you, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't use tracking cookies or analytics tools that follow you around the internet.
  • Bug reports are private. Only our team and the managers we trust can see them.
  • Your location is only added if you allow it, and only as a rough area, never your exact spot.

Who we are

Pyinsect is made by a team of kids for the FIRST LEGO League Bio Glow challenge, with help from grown-ups. It finds invasive insects (bugs that harm nature where they don't belong) and reports them so they can be stopped. "We" and "our team" on this page means the Pyinsect team.

What happens to your photos

The AI that names the insect runs on your own phone or computer. Your photo isn't sent anywhere just to be identified. A photo is only sent to us when you report something (for example, an invasive insect or a damaged tree). Then we save:

  • a smaller copy of the photo (at most 1024 pixels wide, without the hidden camera details inside the file);
  • what the AI thought it was, and how sure it was;
  • the date and time, and which version of Pyinsect you used;
  • a rough area, if you left "Add my rough area" ticked and your browser allowed location (see below).

Reports are locked (encrypted) and saved in a private storage space on GitHub. Only our team and managers can open them. We use them to check the find, and to tell people who protect trees, such as the state agriculture department. We keep reports until our team deletes them.

Your location

  • When you report, your location is rounded to the middle of a square about 75 meters wide before it leaves your device. Only our team sees that.
  • The public Map shows reports in even bigger squares, about 200 meters wide, with only the insect's name and the month. No photos and no names.
  • "Is it near me?" sends your location rounded to about 1 kilometer to the FCC (a U.S. government agency) to find your county. We don't save it.
  • You can untick "Add my rough area", or say no when your browser asks about location. Reports still work.

Accounts and the Innvadex game

You only need an account to play the game. Your account has:

  • a made-up name (like BraveBeetle42) that other players can see;
  • your password, saved only as a scrambled "hash", so even we can't read it;
  • your level (Team, Manager or Player), your avatar, your bugs, and your wins and losses;
  • your friends list, and when you were last online.

Other players can see your made-up name, avatar, bugs, and wins. There is no chat.

Sign in with Google: if you use it, Google tells us a number that stands for your Google account. We keep only a scrambled copy of that number, so we know it's you next time. We don't keep your email, name, or picture from Google. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google does.

Device numbers and blocking

Your browser keeps a random number for this device. It isn't linked to who you are. If someone breaks the rules, our team can block their account and the devices it used. We keep a scrambled copy of the last 20 device numbers for each account. We don't keep IP addresses (your internet address). The servers only count tries for a short time to stop people from guessing passwords, and then forget them.

What's saved in your browser

Pyinsect saves a few things in your browser's storage (not cookies): your settings, reports waiting to be sent, the reports you sent from this device, your game progress, your device number, and your sign-in. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes all of it.

Other services Pyinsect uses

When you use Pyinsect, your browser also talks to these services. Like any website, they can see your internet address while they send you their files:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website. GitHub also stores the locked reports.
  • Cloudflare: runs our two small servers (one gets reports, one runs accounts and the game).
  • Google Fonts: the letters on the page. Google also runs Sign in with Google, only if you use it.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs: code the AI needs to run in your browser.
  • OpenStreetMap: the map pictures.
  • FCC: the county finder (only if you tap "Is it near me?").

Kids

Pyinsect is made by kids and for everyone, including kids. That's why we don't ask for emails, real names, or exact locations. If you're under 13, please use Pyinsect with a parent or teacher, and ask them before you make an account or send a report. Parents can ask us to see or delete anything about their child (see below).

Seeing, fixing, or deleting your information

You (or your parent) can ask us to delete your account or a report you sent. Ask a grown-up on our team, or open an issue on our GitHub page. Only write your made-up name there, never anything private: everyone can read those pages. We'll answer as soon as we can.

Changes

If we change what Pyinsect saves, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
