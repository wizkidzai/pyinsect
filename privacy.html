Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Pyinsect is a free website that looks at a photo of an insect, tells you what it is, and helps report invasive insects (bugs that harm nature where they don't belong). It was made by a team of young students for the FIRST LEGO League challenge, with help from adult coaches. The website is for everyone.

This page explains what information Pyinsect uses, why, and who can see it.

The short version
  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • The AI checks your photos on your own device. Photos are only sent to us when you send a report.
  • Reports can include your rough area, never your exact spot. You can turn this off.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't show ads.

1. Photos

When you take or pick a photo, the AI looks at it on your device. The photo is not uploaded just to check it.

If you send a report about an invasive insect or a sick tree, we send a smaller copy of your photos with it. Before sending, we remove the hidden information that cameras store inside photos (like the exact place where the photo was taken).

Please don't include people's faces, house numbers, or other personal things in your photos.

2. Location

Pyinsect asks to use your location only when you choose to, and you can say no.

  • Your exact location never leaves your device.
  • If you allow it, a report includes a rough area, rounded to a square about 75 meters (250 feet) wide. Only our team and the experts we share reports with can see that area.
  • The public reports map rounds it again, to a square about 200 meters (650 feet) wide. It never shows photos or exact spots.
  • To show which county you're in, we send a rough location (about 1 km) to the U.S. Federal Communications Commission (FCC), a U.S. government agency. It tells us the county name.
  • You can turn off "Add my rough area" before you send a report.

3. Reports

A report includes the photos, what the AI thinks the insect is, the date and time, your answers to the questions, and your rough area if you allow it. It does not include your name or contact details.

Reports are stored in a private place that only our team can open. We may share them with invasive species experts, such as state agriculture or forestry agencies, so they can protect trees and nature.

To stop spam, our server briefly checks your internet (IP) address to limit how many reports can come from one network. We don't save the IP address in your report.

4. The game and online battles

Your game progress is saved on your own device.

If you play online, you get a made-up name (like "BraveBeetle42"). Other players can only see that name, your avatar, your bugs, and your wins. There is no chat. Your device keeps a secret key so the game remembers you. It is not linked to who you are.

5. Information saved on your device

Pyinsect uses your browser's storage to remember things like your game, your last 50 reports, reports waiting to be sent, and your settings. This information stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this website's data in your browser settings.

6. Website analytics

We may use website analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to learn how people use Pyinsect. These tools tell us things like how many people visit, which pages they use, what kind of device they have, and their country or region. This helps us make the website better.

  • Analytics tools may use cookies (small files saved by your browser) or similar technology.
  • We don't use analytics to show ads, and we don't send your photos, reports, or location to them.
  • Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • To opt out, you can block cookies in your browser, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Other services we use

Like most websites, Pyinsect loads some parts from other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies can see basic technical details, like your IP address and browser type.

  • GitHub hosts the website and stores reports in a private place.
  • Cloudflare runs our report server and our online game server.
  • Google Fonts provides the text fonts.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs provide code libraries that run the AI and the map.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map pictures.
  • The FCC finds your county (see section 2).

8. Children

Pyinsect is made for a general audience, and anyone can use it without giving their name or contact details. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please use Pyinsect with a parent, guardian, or teacher, and ask them before you send a report. If you think a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. How long we keep information

We keep reports as long as they help protect nature and support our project. Reports that are waiting on your device are dropped if they can't be sent within 60 days. We delete a report if you ask us to.

10. Your choices

  • Say no to camera or location access. You can still pick photos from your device.
  • Turn off "Add my rough area" before you send a report.
  • Clear this website's data in your browser to delete everything saved on your device.
  • Block cookies or use the opt-out add-on to stop analytics.
  • Ask us to delete a report you sent.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.

12. Contact us

Questions, or want a report deleted? Open an issue on our GitHub page. To delete a report, tell us the date you sent it and what insect it was. Please don't post personal information there, because GitHub issues are public.
