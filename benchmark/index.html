How this test works

  • Pyinsect is our own model, the one the website uses.
  • CLIP is a free general AI made by OpenAI. It was not trained just for insects. We use a smaller, compressed copy.
  • Both get the same photo, cut around the insect by our insect finder, and choose from the same list of insects.
  • The scores count each AI's first guess, even when the website would say "not sure" or "not in my list".
  • A few photos don't prove much. Try 30 or more, and use photos Pyinsect didn't learn from.
  • Your photos stay on your device. Scores are saved only in this browser.
