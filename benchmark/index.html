How this works
  • Pyinsect is our own model, the one the website uses.
  • CLIP is a free general AI made by OpenAI, not trained just for insects. We use a smaller, compressed copy.
  • Both get the same photo, cut around the insect, and choose from the same list of insects.
  • Scores count each AI's first guess, even when the website would say "not sure".
  • Try 30 or more photos that Pyinsect didn't learn from.
  • Your photos stay on your device. Scores are saved only in this browser.
